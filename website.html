Our Story

At HereBags, luxury is redefined through artistry and intention. Handmade in Fairtrade workshops in South America using sustainable, cruelty-free materials of the highest quality, proving that luxury and compassion can exist side by side.

Every bag embodies more than craftmanship. It embodies exclusivity. Each bag is produced in limited quntities, often arriving in Australia as one of a kind, single piece, or limited edition, ensuring your bag is as rare as it is refined.

This is why carrying a HereBag is not simply about fashion, carrying what you need, it is about carrying who you are.

As the sole authorised distributor of Hereje and HerejeWear in Australia and New Zealand, HereBags offers conscious luxury you would not find anywhere else.
